What We Do

Three service streams. One engineering partner.

Each stream works independently or combines for a fully integrated engagement — from advisory through to volume production.

01

Engineering Consultancy

Ongoing technical support across your active projects
  • On-site and remote review of engineering projects
  • Technical checks on designs, drawings & tolerances
  • Supplier and subcontractor follow-up
  • Machine commissioning & performance testing
  • Written reports with findings & recommendations
02

R&D & Machine Design

From idea to a fully documented design — ready to build
  • Market benchmarking & competitive analysis
  • 2–3 concept designs with clear recommendation
  • Full mechanical design: structure, drives, spindle
  • Engineering drawings, 3D CAD & complete BOM
  • Prototype coordination, testing & validation
03

OEM Manufacturing

We build the machines — you sell them under your brand
  • Complete CNC machines to your specifications
  • Sub-assemblies: frames, gantries, drive modules
  • Component sourcing & qualification
  • QC inspection on every unit with written records
  • Packaging, labeling & delivery coordination

Capabilities & Tools
CAD / CAM

SolidWorks, AutoCAD, Fusion 360. Full 3D modeling, assembly design, and manufacturing-ready drawings.

Controllers

Fanuc (0i-MD, 0i-MF), LinuxCNC with Mesa hardware, Mach3/4. Servo and stepper configurations.

Drive Systems

Ball screws, linear rails (THK, Hiwin, PMI), harmonic drives, servo motors (Yaskawa, Delta), VFDs.

Embedded

Arduino, ESP32, RS485 networks. Custom firmware for motion control, automation, and safety systems.

Analysis

FEA structural analysis, backlash measurement, spindle runout testing, geometric verification.

Manufacturing

CNC milling & turning, precision scraping, assembly, wiring, commissioning, and QC inspection.
